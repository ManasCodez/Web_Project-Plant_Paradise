Medicinal Plants

Aloe Vera

A succulent plant with thick, fleshy leaves.

Famous for: Skincare and healing burns

Uses: Treats sunburns, moisturizes skin, helps with minor cuts.

Tulsi

A sacred herb grown in many Indian homes.

Famous for: Improving immunity and helping coughs/colds

Uses: Brewed as tea, fights infections, relieves cold symptoms.

Neem

A tree known for its bitter leaves and wide uses.

Famous for: Antibacterial and skincare properties

Uses: Used in soaps, treats skin problems, repels insects.

Mint

A refreshing herb with a strong smell and taste.

Famous for: Digestion and refreshing flavor

Uses: Added to drinks, helps digestion, freshens breath.

Edible Plants

Spinach

A green leafy vegetable eaten worldwide.

Famous for: Rich in nutrients and iron

Uses: Added to salads, soups, and smoothies for nutrition.

Carrot

A bright orange root vegetable, crunchy and sweet.

Famous for: Vitamin A and eye health

Uses: Eaten raw, in soups, juices, and salads.

Broccoli

A green tree-like vegetable with a mild taste.

Famous for: Its nutritional value

Uses: Steamed, boiled, stir-fried, or eaten raw.

Potato

A starchy tuber eaten in many dishes around the world.

Famous for: Commonly used as a food vegetable

Uses: Fried, boiled, baked, mashed, and used in curries.

Indoor Plants

Snake Plant

A tall, upright plant with long sword-like leaves.

Famous for: Being low maintenance

Uses: Purifies air, needs little water, good for beginners.

Money Plant

A popular trailing plant with round green leaves.

Famous for: Being a decorative indoor plant

Uses: Decorates homes, purifies air, easy to grow in water.

Spider Plant

A plant with long arching leaves and small white flowers.

Famous for: Easy to grow at home

Uses: Removes indoor pollutants, easy to care for, good for beginners.

Peace Lily

A plant with dark green leaves and beautiful white flowers.

Famous for: Beautiful white flowers and indoor appearance

Uses: Decorates rooms, improves air quality, thrives in shade.

Flowering Plants

Rose

A classic flower with a sweet fragrance and many colors.

Famous for: A symbol of love and beauty

Uses: Used in bouquets, perfumes, weddings, and decorations.

Sunflower

A large bright yellow flower that follows the sun.

Famous for: Its large yellow flower

Uses: Grown for seeds, oil, gardens, and decorations.

Hibiscus

A tropical plant with large colorful flowers.

Famous for: Its colorful flowers

Uses: Used in tea, decorations, and gardens.

Jasmine

A vine with small white flowers and a strong sweet smell.

Famous for: Its strong and pleasant fragrance

Uses: Used in perfumes, tea, garlands, and aromatherapy.

Fruit Plants

Mango

A tropical fruit tree that produces sweet yellow fruit.

Famous for: The "King of Fruits"

Uses: Eaten fresh, in juices, desserts, pickles, and smoothies.

Banana

A long curved fruit with soft sweet flesh inside.

Famous for: A quick source of energy

Uses: Eaten as a snack, in smoothies, desserts, and cooking.

Orange

A round citrus fruit with a juicy and sour-sweet taste.

Famous for: Being rich in Vitamin C

Uses: Eaten fresh, squeezed for juice, and used in cooking.

Grapes

Small round fruits that grow in clusters on vines.

Famous for: Sweet taste and use in many food products

Uses: Eaten fresh, dried as raisins, and used to make wine and juice.

Desert Plants / Succulents

Cactus

A spiky plant that stores water in its thick stem.

Famous for: Surviving with very little water

Uses: Grown as decorative plants, low water needs, easy care.

Aloe Vera

A thick-leaved succulent with a clear gel inside.

Famous for: Medicinal uses

Uses: Treats burns, used in skincare, and made into juice.

Jade Plant

A succulent with thick, tree-like stems and round leaves.

Famous for: Being a popular decorative succulent

Uses: Grown as a houseplant, symbolizes good luck.

Agave

A large succulent with thick, pointed leaves.

Famous for: Thick leaves and surviving dry conditions

Uses: Used for tequila, fibers, and as a garden plant.
